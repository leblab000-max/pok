# Secondary pooled ablations

Equal-profile differences averaged within each of 72 seed clusters. These are exploratory, not additional primary claims. Two contrasts, Bonferroni alpha=.05/2, 10000 replicates. No model is reselected from this report.

| Contrast | Mean ROI difference pp | Adjusted interval pp |
|---|---:|---|
| adaptation on minus off | 19.3 | [-8.3, 46.2] |
| continuation on minus off | 38.3 | [16.7, 59.8] |

The selected fixed candidate uses Mathematical as its base. Consequently no_rollout exactly matches Mathematical on all 216 shared policy/population/seed scenarios. This verifies the intervention but does not create a new independent baseline. Normal within-decision rollout bounds at eight samples cover neither model misspecification nor all selection effects. Tournament outcomes are the confirmation evidence.
